# ClinicalCore

Free clinical calculators that show their working. They run entirely in the browser, work offline, and send nothing.

**Use it:** https://nabvian.github.io/clinicalcore/

- ObiLogic: obstetric history (GTPAL) and antenatal card reader
- Death Certificate Check: cause-of-death wording against WHO rules
- Antenatal Check: visits, tests, Td and high-risk factors (ICMR, MCP card)
- Vaccine Catch-up: India national schedule from birth to 16 years
- Corrected Age: for babies born preterm

Every rule is taken from an official guideline and cited on each page.

**Not clinically signed off or validated.** Check every result against your local protocol. If you think anything should change, [share your thoughts](https://github.com/nabvian/clinicalcore/issues/new?template=feedback.md). We reply within 1–2 weeks.

This repository holds the published site only.
