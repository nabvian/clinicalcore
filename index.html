ClinicalCore

Clinical calculators that show their working

Each tool records the inputs, refuses to guess when something is missing, and produces a note you can paste into any record. Every rule is taken from an official guideline and cited.

Early release: built from published guidelines, but not clinically signed off or validated. Check every result against your local protocol. If you think anything should change, share your thoughts. We reply within 1–2 weeks.

Obstetrics

ObiLogic

Reads the antenatal card (G3P2L1A1, LSCS 2021…), checks it against each pregnancy, and flags what matters. English, हिन्दी, বাংলা. Works offline.
Mortality

Death Certificate Check

Checks Form 4/4A before it is signed: modes of dying, vague causes, sequence, manner of death, pregnancy questions. Audits many certificates at once. Registrar General and WHO rules.
Antenatal care

Antenatal Check

Visits, tests, GDM, Td and medicines due for this week of pregnancy, plus high-risk factors. ICMR workflow and MCP card.
Immunisation

Vaccine Catch-up

India UIP from birth to 16 years, by the 2024 Routine Immunization Manual: what to give today, what is late, and a session due list with doses to bring.
