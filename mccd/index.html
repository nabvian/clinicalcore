ClinicalCoreDeath Certificate Check

Write the cause-of-death section as you plan to certify it. This checks the wording and the sequence only. It never suggests a diagnosis.

Part 1: chain of events leading to death

Line (a) is the condition that led directly to death. Each line below is what caused the line above it ("due to"). The lowest line used is the underlying cause.

Part 2: other significant conditions

Deceased and manner of death

As on Form 4 (hospital) and Form 4A (other deaths). Neither form is used for stillbirths.
